# Continuation Betting

A continuation bet is a flop bet by the preflop aggressor. It works when the board, ranges, and opponent tendencies support the story.

Daniel's c-betting approach is not automatic. Some boards favor the raiser. Some boards smash the caller. Some opponents fold too much. Others float every flop and make the turn hard.

Good c-bet boards:

- Ace-high dry boards for the preflop raiser.
- Paired boards where the caller rarely connects strongly.
- Dry king-high boards after strong preflop action.

Boards that need caution:

- Low connected boards.
- Two-tone boards with many draws.
- Boards that hit the caller's suited connectors and small pairs.
- Multiway pots, if added later.

Sizing should match texture. Small c-bets can pressure dry boards efficiently. Larger bets make sense when charging draws or betting polarized ranges.

Common mistakes:

- C-betting every flop.
- Giving up every turn after being called.
- Betting medium-strength hands too large.
- Ignoring how the board interacts with both ranges.

Good Daniel-style takeaway: "A c-bet is not a receipt you get for raising preflop, buddy. The flop still gets a vote."
